Security Center

This is a front-end demo, so there's no real account to protect — but it still follows good client-side security habits:

  • All form input is validated and length-limited before it's used anywhere
  • Text is escaped before being inserted into the page, to block basic script-injection attempts
  • A Content-Security-Policy restricts which sources scripts/styles/fonts can load from
  • Freezing your card requires a confirmation step before it takes effect
  • Card number and CVV stay masked by default

Help Center

Account & Security

Go to Me → tap your tier badge (or "Upgrade" next to KYC Level) to open the Verification Center. Each tier needs a bit more ID information in exchange for higher transaction limits.
Use "Forgot password?" on the Log In screen. In this demo build that flow isn't wired to a real email sender yet — connect it to your real password-reset service when you have one.
Turn on every toggle in Me → Security Center, never share your PIN or OTP with anyone (DubemPay staff will never ask for it), and set up Security Questions in Settings.

Transfers & Payments

Most transfers complete in under a minute. If a recipient bank is having network issues, it can take longer — check Transfer Success Rate (inside "To Bank") before sending to see which banks are running slow.
Transfers to another DubemPay wallet ("To Wallet") are always free. Bank transfers ("To Bank") and withdrawals may carry a small fee depending on the destination.

Savings & Cards

OWealth is for everyday overflow — deposit and withdraw anytime. SafeBox pays a higher rate in exchange for a withdrawal schedule you commit to (Quarterly or Monthly), with a small breaking fee if you withdraw early.
